🌟 I Can Do It! 🌟

Learning about CAN and CAN'T

What does "CAN" mean? ✅

"CAN" means you are able to do something!

🏃

I can run!

🦘

I can jump!

💃

I can dance!

🏊

I can swim!

What does "CAN'T" mean? ❌

"CAN'T" (or CANNOT) means you are NOT able to do something!

🚫✈️

I can't fly!

🚫🚗

I can't drive a car!

🐟🚫🚶

Fish can't walk!

🐱🚫

Cats can't bark!

📐 Sentence Pattern

Subject + can / can't + verb

✔️ I can sing

❌ Dogs can't talk
